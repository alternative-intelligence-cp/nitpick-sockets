## 1. What was measured — the facts this plan rests on

At compiler `2e1cb4a` with LLVM 20.1.8 on 2026-10-25, by [`0.0.4_tools/facts.sh`](0.0.4_tools/facts.sh) over the
checkout at 0.0.3's record and a throwaway clone with the three patches applied — never in the tree. **The worker
re-derives each with block 0b** and stops on any difference. Each program `facts.sh` writes carries the units'
`failsafe`, whose arms are `PLAYBOOK.md` §3 item 8's codes: 91 `HeapBadRequest`, 92 `HeapOom`, 93 `IntOverflow`, 94
`OutOfBounds`, 95 `Unreachable`, 96 `WildLeak`, 109 `LimitViolated`, 116 `RequiresViolated`, 117 `EnsuresViolated`.

### 1.1 The tree it starts from

0.0.3's record: `src/core/` holds its placeholder alone, `tests/unit/` no unit, the manifest four entries, and
`check_constants_named` reads no `src/core/limits.npk` and says so.

### 1.2 The bounds, and the first writing's two lists

The first writing's cycle README named four bounds "and the deadline defaults"; its 0.0.4.md nine. The specifications
state three as `` `NSOCK_<NAME>` = n `` once step 1 spells C-11's value so — `NSOCK_CMSG_MAX` = 256 (N-4),
`NSOCK_SCM_MAX_FDS` = 16 (N-5), `NSOCK_EMFILE_BACKOFF` = 50 (C-11, which reads "(a named constant, 50 ms)" as the tree
stands) — and name `NSOCK_POLLSET_MAX` (C-13) with no value; cycle 0.8's checklist names it. The first writing's other
five appear in no specification.

### 1.3 The value rule, against the kernel's values

16 is also `SO_PASSCRED` (N-14), and the stated size of `cmsghdr`, `iovec` and `sockaddr_in`; 50 is `listen`'s number
in K-1's table; 256 is no number a specification states. Over a planted tree — the three bounds in `limits.npk`,
`src/sys/` declaring `SO_PASSCRED = 16` and `SYS_LISTEN = 50`, `src/addr/` passing `sockaddr_in`'s size as the literal 16
— 0.0.3's `check_constants_named` reports three findings, one per literal, and the patched tree's none, holding
`NSOCK_CMSG_MAX` by value and the other two by name.

### 1.4 What an index does

At both legs: a read at index 9 of a `wild` block of four `int64`s, and at index 9 of a four-byte buffer's `.ptr`,
answers — exit 0; past the end of a slice, of a fixed array and of a buffer, and of a buffer read or written through a
pointer parameter's field, traps `OutOfBounds`, 94. A range of a buffer, `b[0i64...4i64]`, held in a block: a byte of
the buffer written through its index while the view lives compiles; the buffer overwritten in the view's block after its
last use is `NITPICK-BORROW-015`, and so is the same overwrite done by a callee handed the holder's address.

### 1.5 What a live clause and a division cost, and what each traps

A module whose one function carries a live `requires`, an `ensures`, or builds a struct with a `limit<…>` field charges
a consumer seven identities — the floor's six and `RequiresViolated`, `EnsuresViolated` or `LimitViolated`; a module
that divides by a literal charges eight, `DivByZero` and `DivOverflow` beside the six, and so does a module that only
imports it. Violated at run time, each clause traps its arm at both legs: 116, 117, 109. **And `vec_at` at `i == count`
exits 94 with its obligation a comment, 116 with the obligation live**: a live `requires` pre-empts the body's check
(`PLAYBOOK.md` §3 item 10's measurement, re-taken here on this library's accessor).

### 1.6 The specification's spellings — Z-2, Z-4 and Z-5

Z-2's shape, given a body for its `…`, compiles, and so does the same with `never fails` before `requires`, the order
the obligation comments write. Z-4's block as written is `NITPICK-RESOLVE-002` at `ntoh16`, declared nowhere yet; its
first two shapes without the third are `NITPICK-TYPE-001` twice, for `SocketAddr` and `UnixAddr`, and compile as written,
without a body, once those two types are declared; the third, with `ntoh16` declared, is `NITPICK-TYPE-060` whether
`ntoh16` is plain or `pure`, or plain and called `raw`, and compiles with `ntoh16` `pure never fails` and called `raw
ntoh16(result)`. Z-5's first rule as written is `NITPICK-PARSE-001`; `Rules<uint16>:Port = { $ >= 0u16, $ <= 65535u16 };`
compiles. Step 2's notes on Z-4 and Z-5 say exactly this.

### 1.7 The containers' shapes, from a unit's place

A copy of a `Vec<int64>` and of a `Bytes` is `NITPICK-TYPE-046`; a struct of `Vec`'s three other fields copies, so the
fourth, `hidden string[0]:move_only`, is what makes a `Vec` move-only — and `Vec<int64>` is 24 bytes, the size of those
three. A read of `items` and of `buf` from another module is `NITPICK-TYPE-080`; a write of `count` and of `len`,
`NITPICK-TYPE-079`, and a read of `count` and `cap` compiles. `Vec<string>` is `NITPICK-TYPE-017` at each of its three
sites; a generic function over `Vec<T>` without the `T: Copy` bound is `NITPICK-TYPE-017` twice, and with it compiles
and runs. `NULL` into a plain pointer field is `NITPICK-TYPE-096`; into a `wild` one it compiles (D-355).

### 1.8 The allocator's ceiling, and the literal envelope

`vec_init` of 2^44 + 1 `int64`s — 2^47 + 8 bytes — traps `HeapBadRequest`, 91, at both legs; of 2^44, exactly 2^47
bytes, under a 4096 MiB cap, `HeapOom`, 92: the refusal is the allocator's 2^47 ceiling, not memory. `int64`'s maximum
spelled as a literal compiles; its minimum, any `uint64` past `int64`'s maximum, 10^19, and `uint64`'s maximum are
`NITPICK-LEX-004`.

### 1.9 Every obligation made live

[`obligations.py`](0.0.4_tools/obligations.py) replaces each function's `never fails {` by its comment's clause and
compiles the module alone: all 24 — `vec.npk`'s 12 and `bytes.npk`'s 12, one per function — compile live.

### 1.10 The generic instances

`vec.npk` compiled alone defines, of its own functions, the two that are not generic, `vec_grown` and `vec_oob`;
`tests/unit/generic_instances.npk`'s emission defines ten instances, one per generic function, as
`npk.vec.vec_at<int64>`.

### 1.11 What a consumer of each module of `src/core/` owes

`limits.npk` six identities, the floor's; `vec.npk` and `bytes.npk` eight each, the six with `IntOverflow` and
`OutOfBounds`; the same `bytes.npk` with one function that takes a digit by `%`, ten, `DivByZero` and `DivOverflow`
beside them, and ten again to a consumer of a module that imports it.

### 1.12 Allocation

A thousand renderings, 0 … 999, appended into a `Bytes` made with room for all 2890 digits: by `bytes_put_uint`, the
runtime's `NPK_HEAP_STATS` counts one allocation, the sink; by `int_to_string` and `bytes_extend_str`, 1001.

### 1.13 G8's plant

`int64:g8_planted = (k - k) + 9223372036854775807i64 + 1i64;` first in a loop's body traps `IntOverflow`, 93, at both
legs; an `exit` in a function other than `main` and `failsafe` is `NITPICK-TYPE-010`, so the plant overflows instead.

### 1.14 The shapes, the rulings and the names

### 1.15 The safety rules, read against every `.npk` the patches add
